# Open Questions & Decision Points for Client Sign-Off

This document compiles the strategic, operational, and regulatory questions for founder **Zulaid Chaudhary** and the foundation leadership prior to commencing Phase 1 application implementation.

---

## 1. Legal Entity & Regulatory Status
1. **Official Registered Name:** Is the formal filing name *"Zulaid Chaudhary Foundation"*, or is there an affiliated trust/society name (e.g., registered under the Societies Registration Act 1860 or Trust Act)?
2. **Current Regulatory Milestone:** What is the exact status of the registration application with the relevant authorities (e.g., Punjab Charities Commission / Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan / FBR Section 2(36))?
   - *Default Setting:* We have configured the site to display *"Registration in progress"* with a neutral compliance badge until official certificate details are supplied.
3. **Tax Deductibility Disclaimers:** Should donation receipts explicitly state that donations are not yet tax-deductible until FBR approval is finalized? (We strongly recommend this transparency).

---

## 2. Lahore Charity Hospital Specifications
1. **Hospital Project Location & Name:** What is the official project title (e.g., *"Zulaid Chaudhary Charity Hospital Lahore"*), and what is its specific district/neighborhood in Lahore?
2. **Planned Bed Capacity & Floors:** What is the planned scale of the facility (e.g., 50-bed, 100-bed, or multi-specialty 4-story facility)?
3. **Priority Clinical Departments:** Which departments will open in Phase 1 (e.g., General Surgery, Outpatient / Free Dispensary, Dialysis, Pediatric Care, Emergency)?
4. **Architectural Renders & Photos:** Are there formal architectural blueprints, 3D renders, or on-site construction photos available to feature in the interactive timeline component?

---

## 3. Medical Network & Doctor Credentials
1. **Family Physician Credentials:** To showcase the medical advisory strength, what titles, specialties, and affiliations may be stated for the family doctors in the USA and Lahore?
   - *Rule Check:* Per `AGENTS.md`, titles and positions will remain unlisted or marked pending until each physician provides written confirmation.
2. **Current Patient Treatment Arrangements:** For the approximately 25-30 patients who received free surgical/clinical care, which partner hospitals or clinics in Lahore performed these procedures?

---

## 4. Banking & Donation Gateways (For Phase 3 Readiness)
1. **Pakistani Bank Account:** Which domestic bank will receive offline wire transfers (e.g., Meezan Bank, Habib Bank Limited, Bank Alfalah), and what is the official Title of Account, IBAN, and Branch Code?
2. **Raast Instant QR ID:** Does the foundation possess a dedicated Raast Merchant / Corporate ID or IBAN-linked phone identifier?
3. **Stripe & International Card Processing:** Does the foundation or founder hold an authorized international corporate entity (e.g., US 501(c)(3) or UK charity branch) to connect Stripe for credit card donations in USD/GBP/EUR, or will international card payments remain in Test Mode initially?

---

## 5. Contact Channels & Emergency Escalation
1. **Dedicated Emergency Helpline:** What phone number should be configured for the public emergency medical assistance inquiry bar?
2. **Official WhatsApp Business Number:** What phone number will be dedicated to donor WhatsApp interactions and bank wire slip confirmations?
3. **Physical Headquarters Address:** What office address in Lahore should be published in the global footer and regulatory filings?

---

## 6. Language & Content Localization
1. **Urdu Tone & Style:** Do you prefer formal literary Urdu (*Khas Urdu*) or modern accessible conversational Urdu for articles and advocacy briefings?
2. **Proofreading Sign-off:** We will generate complete, authentic Urdu translations for all UI components and core pages. Who will serve as the final human proofreader on the client side before live deployment?
